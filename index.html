Who I Am

Researcher by Training,
Editor by Practice

I am an MSc student in the Department of Soil, Water and Environment at the University of Dhaka (CGPA 3.60/4.00) and a recipient of the National Science and Technology Fellowship. My thesis investigates bentonite-biochar coated urea as a climate-smart slow-release fertilizer for South Asian cropping systems.

Alongside my thesis, I hold active research roles at BCSIR (atmospheric PM characterisation and heavy metal analysis) and recently completed a nano-fertilizer experiment at DUNTC. I also built SPADE — an open-source Python platform for standardised nitrogen use efficiency analysis.

Three Active Fronts

— ❧ —

Soil fertility, nano-technology, and urban air quality — simultaneous research across Bangladesh's leading institutions.

MSc Thesis · Featured

Bentonite-Biochar Coated Urea & Nitrogen Use Efficiency

Synthesising BBCU from rice husk biochar (pyrolysed at 550°C), sodium bentonite, and corn starch binder. N-release kinetics modelled via Higuchi and Korsmeyer-Peppas equations. Running soil incubation, 50 cm leaching columns, and jar-based volatilisation experiments simultaneously.

Full Research Details
🌾 DUNTC · Nano Fertilizer

Nano Urea vs. Conventional Urea on Wheat

24-pot CRD experiment on BARI Gom 33. Comprehensive NUE indices, Kjeldahl N, tissue nutrient quality (AAS + ICP-OES), and post-harvest soil dynamics despite a hailstorm mid-experiment.

💨 BCSIR · Atmospheric Science

PM₀.₁ & Heavy Metal Characterisation

Ultrafine particulate matter sampling with Nanosampler II (Kanomax 3182) and heavy metal analysis via AAS in Dhaka's urban environment. Ecological risk index assessment ongoing.

Manuscript Editing
& Formatting

From draft to journal-ready — your ideas, professionally presented.

Research papers succeed or fail on clarity, structure, and presentation. I provide thorough language correction, structural editing, and journal-specific formatting for manuscripts targeting Elsevier, MDPI, Wiley, Springer, and similar publishers. I work with your existing writing — improving how your ideas are expressed, not replacing them with someone else's voice.

My own training in scientific communication — Stanford Writing in the Sciences and Wiley Researcher Academy certification — informs every edit I make. I specialise in soil science, environmental science, agriculture, and sustainability, but work across all natural and applied sciences.

What's included
  • Full manuscript read-through and structural assessment

  • Journal-specific formatting (headings, references, figures)

Data Analysis
& Statistics

Numbers that tell the right story.

Good data poorly analysed is a wasted opportunity. I provide statistical analysis of research datasets using SPSS, R, and Python — selecting the appropriate tests for your design, running the analysis correctly, producing clear figures, and writing up the results in plain academic language. I also built SPADE, an open-source Python platform for nitrogen use efficiency analysis, so I know what rigorous quantitative research looks like from the inside.

I work across all research areas — not just soil science. If you have a dataset and need it analysed and interpreted, send it over and we can discuss the right approach.

What's included
  • Data cleaning and preparation (handling missing values, outliers)
  • Appropriate statistical tests selected and justified
  • Full analysis run in SPSS, R, or Python — your choice
